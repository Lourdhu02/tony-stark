    Papers: the master index#

    Every work cited anywhere in this repo: 266 works (217 papers, 39 books, 10 specs, courses, talks and essays), spanning 1927–2025, cited across 26 folders.

    Each folder has its own annotated REFERENCES.md: why each work matters there and what to read. This page is the index: chronological, with back-links.

    How to read a paper#

    S. Keshav's three-pass method (How to read a paper, 2007), adapted:

    Pass Time Do Stop if
    1 · Survey 10 min title, abstract, intro, headings, conclusion; glance at the figures it's not relevant to the Mark you're on
    2 · Grasp 1 h read everything except proofs; annotate the figures; list the references you'd chase you can summarize the contribution in 3 sentences
    3 · Re-create 4–5 h re-derive the key result, or re-implement it, from memory never: this is the pass that makes it yours

    Write the 3-sentence summary in that week's lab note. Research-grade (★★★) papers usually need pass 3 before they click.
