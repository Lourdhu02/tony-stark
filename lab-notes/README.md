# lab-notes/

> The note is how you know the week happened.

One file per ISO week, named `YYYY-Www.md` (for example `2026-W41.md`), copied from [`TEMPLATE.md`](TEMPLATE.md).

## Why bother

| Benefit | Because |
|---|---|
| **Learning** | Explaining the week's hardest idea in your own words is the Feynman test. The gaps show up mid-sentence. |
| **Memory** | Six months from now, "why did I pick first-fit?" is answered in one search. |
| **Proof** | The notes are a public, dated record of growth, which is worth more in interviews than any certificate. |
| **Honesty** | The *Numbers* section keeps "I think it's faster" out of your vocabulary. |

## What a good note looks like

- **Built:** links to commits, not descriptions of them.
- **Learned:** one concept explained properly, with a diagram or equation if it helps.
- **Broke:** the bug, the root cause and the fix. This is the most valuable section.
- **Numbers:** test counts, benchmark results and the exit criteria still remaining.
- **Next week:** three tasks at most. More than three means none of them happen.

Keep it to 30 minutes. A short note every week beats a long one every month.
