# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This is a place for learning hard things. **Asking a "basic" question is always welcome.**

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback: on the work, never the person
- helping learners find the answer rather than handing it over (see the lab's one rule in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md))
- taking responsibility for mistakes, and correcting errata openly

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/Lourdhu02) or by opening a confidential security advisory on this repository. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled in confidence. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for harmful behaviour.
